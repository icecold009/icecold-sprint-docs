# Hackathon submission draft

## Project name

IceCold Sprint

## Problem

Students often know what they need to study but do not know how to prioritize it when an exam is close. Generic chat interfaces produce advice, but they do not reliably turn time, confidence, and topic importance into an actionable sprint.

## Target user

Students preparing for an exam within one or two days who need a focused revision plan and immediate practice.

## Solution

IceCold Sprint creates a time-bounded study plan, provides topic quizzes, evaluates performance, and recommends the next best study action.

## End-to-end workflow

1. Select Physics, Biology, Math, Chemistry, or History, or add a custom subject.
2. Review and customize topics and confidence levels.
3. Generate a prioritized study plan.
4. Complete a five-question topic quiz.
5. See a score and adaptive next recommendation.
6. Continue the sprint with progress saved locally.
